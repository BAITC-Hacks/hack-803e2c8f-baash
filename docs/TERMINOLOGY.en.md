## Names and boundaries

Smart Intake, Emerging Issues Radar, Incident War Room, Ask Pulse, Operations Center, Data Lab, Replay Lab, Outcome Memory, Next Best Action and Decision Gateway are product names shared by all languages. PostgreSQL, PostGIS, pgvector, FastAPI, Next.js, MapLibre, Docker, API, SLA, PII and RU/EN/KK are also preserved.

An appeal and an incident are distinct entities. AI proposes; a human confirms. Synthetic demo records are neither city statistics nor evidence of model quality. Translation preserves these boundaries.
